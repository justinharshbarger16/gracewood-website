    What the layout generator considers

    • Guest count translated into the exact number of round or long tables required
    • Open circulation from the main entrance into the event floor
    • Room access to the kitchen/bar side, bridal suite, and restrooms
    • Buffet, cookie table, sweetheart table, and ceremony setup placed where they naturally fit
