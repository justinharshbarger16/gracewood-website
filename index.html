    About Gracewood

    About Gracewood

    Gracewood is a venue created for meaningful celebrations in the Pittsburgh region. Our vision is to provide a space that feels beautiful, flexible, and approachable for weddings, showers, parties, fundraisers, corporate gatherings, and other memorable occasions.

    We believe every event is unique. Our approach is rooted in communication, customization, and helping clients feel supported from the first inquiry through the event itself. We want every host to feel confident that their vision can come to life here.

    At Gracewood, our goal is simple: provide a refined event setting, an organized planning experience, and a welcoming atmosphere that helps every celebration feel exceptional.

    Mission

    Our mission is to serve the community by providing a unique and versatile event space for meaningful celebrations. We aim to exceed expectations through communication, flexibility, and personalized planning support.

    Positioning

    Community-oriented, customizable, polished, client-focused, welcoming, and elevated but approachable.
